Somalia — The Sea, the Sun, and the Smiles

They love to smile — and to chew khat. Maybe that’s how they make a hard life a little more bearable.
A country shaped by struggle, yet filled with warmth. Somalis smile easily — maybe because the ocean teaches calm, maybe because life’s lighter when you share it. After long field days, we always took a detour to the shore — engine off, music on, five quiet minutes with the Indian Ocean. 🌊
